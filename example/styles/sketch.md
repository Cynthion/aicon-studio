# Sketch

Create a minimal UI icon of {subject} in a modern hand-drawn style, as if drawn with a black felt-tip marker on a whiteboard.

## Look

- Use one even marker line with a slight, natural wobble and lightly rounded ends; human, but neat and confident, never messy.
- Keep the line weight consistent, as in one cohesive icon set.
- Keep the icon simple, balanced, highly legible and vector-friendly, with a clear silhouette, minimal internal detail and no tiny fragile details.
- Centered on a square canvas, filling about 80% of it, suitable for SVG and small icon sizes such as 24 px.

## Outlines

- Draw outlines only: every shape is an empty outline with a white inside, never a solid black area, except for dots.
- The icon must also work filled solid later, so draw every outer contour, including every circle, ring and frame, as one continuous closed line with no gap and no break: where a line ends, it meets its start exactly.
- A symbol made of single strokes, such as a check mark, an arrow or a character, stays a set of strokes.

## Colour And Limits

- Single colour: solid black on a transparent background.
- No shading, no hatching, no scribbles, no doubled or overlapping sketch lines, no gradients, no 3D, no texture, no frame, no background, no words or captions.
- Avoid photorealism, cartoon mascots, thick comic outlines and excessive detail.
