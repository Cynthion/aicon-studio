# Monoline

Create a minimal UI icon of {subject} in a clean geometric monoline style, as used in modern web applications.

## Look

- Build every shape from simple geometric primitives: circles, straight lines, arcs and rounded rectangles.
- Use one uniform medium stroke weight everywhere, with rounded line caps, rounded joins and a small consistent corner radius, as in one cohesive icon set.
- Keep the icon simple, balanced, highly legible and vector-friendly, with a clear silhouette, minimal internal detail and no tiny fragile details.
- Align lines to an invisible 24 by 24 grid and keep straight lines horizontal, vertical or at 45 degrees where the subject allows.
- Centered on a square canvas, filling about 80% of it, suitable for SVG and small icon sizes such as 24 px.

## Outlines

- Draw outlines only: every shape is an empty outline with a white inside, never a solid black area, except for dots.
- The icon must also work filled solid later, so draw every outer contour, including every circle, ring and frame, as one continuous closed line with no gap and no break.
- A symbol made of single strokes, such as a check mark, an arrow or a character, stays a set of strokes.

## Colour And Limits

- Single colour: solid black on a transparent background.
- No shading, no gradients, no 3D, no texture, no frame, no background, no words or captions.
- Avoid photorealism, hand-drawn wobble, varying line weight, cartoon mascots and decorative detail.
